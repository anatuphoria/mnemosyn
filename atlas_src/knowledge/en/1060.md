## Function
The cortex of the operculum around the lower end of the central sulcus, covering the insula. It holds the lower parts of the motor and sensory maps, for the face, mouth and throat, and areas for taste and speech articulation [purves2018].

## Connections
It is linked with the adjacent motor, sensory and insular cortex and with the ventral posteromedial nucleus of the thalamus [kandel2021].
