## Function
A group of nuclei around the stria terminalis near the anterior commissure, often grouped with the amygdala. It takes part in sustained anxiety and in the stress response [ulrichlai2009].

## Connections
It receives from the amygdala and hippocampus and projects to the paraventricular nucleus of the hypothalamus and to brainstem autonomic centres [ulrichlai2009].
