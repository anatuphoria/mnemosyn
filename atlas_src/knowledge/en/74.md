## Function
The long tracts that run through the brainstem, carrying sensation up to the thalamus and motor commands down to the spinal cord [purves2018].

## Connections
They include the medial and lateral lemnisci, the spinothalamic tract, the central tegmental tract, the medial longitudinal fasciculus and the rubrospinal tract [kandel2021].
