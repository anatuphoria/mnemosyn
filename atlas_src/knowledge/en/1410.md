## Function
A C shaped nucleus that follows the lateral ventricle. With the putamen it forms the striatum, the input stage of the basal ganglia; the caudate takes part mainly in eye movements, learning and goal directed behaviour [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the prefrontal and association cortex and from dopamine neurons of the substantia nigra, and projects to the globus pallidus and substantia nigra pars reticulata [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The caudate shrinks early in Huntington disease, which causes involuntary dance like movements (chorea) [purves2018].
