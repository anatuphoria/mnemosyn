## Function
The frontal cortex supplied by the middle cerebral artery: the lateral frontal lobe, including the motor cortex for the face and arm and, in the dominant hemisphere, Broca's area [purves2018].

## Connections
Its branches come from the upper division of the middle cerebral artery [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct here causes weakness of the opposite face and arm and, in the dominant hemisphere, Broca's aphasia [purves2018].
