## Function
The fibres from the frontal cortex to the pontine nuclei. They carry plans for movement and other frontal signals to the cerebellum [kandel2021].

## Connections
They run through the anterior limb of the internal capsule and the medial part of the cerebral peduncle and end in the pontine nuclei, which project to the opposite cerebellum [kandel2021].
