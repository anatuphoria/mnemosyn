## Function
The main source of noradrenaline (norepinephrine) for the forebrain, cerebellum and spinal cord; its activity is linked to arousal, attention and the sleep-wake cycle [purves2018].

## Connections
A small nucleus in the rostral pons near the floor of the fourth ventricle, whose highly branched axons reach most of the central nervous system [kandel2021].
