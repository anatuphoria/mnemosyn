## Function
The front part of the spinal grey matter, holding the motor neurons that drive the skeletal muscles. It is larger in the segments for the arms and legs [purves2018].

## Connections
Its motor neurons receive from the corticospinal and brainstem tracts and from sensory fibres in the stretch reflex, and send their axons out in the ventral roots [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Loss of its motor neurons, as in poliomyelitis or motor neuron disease, causes weakness with wasting and twitching of the muscles [purves2018].
