## Function
The narrow middle part of the cerebellum. It controls posture, walking and eye movements, through the fastigial nucleus and the vestibular nuclei [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the spinal cord, vestibular system and visual system and projects through the fastigial nucleus to the vestibular nuclei and reticular formation [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes an unsteady trunk and gait while limb movements stay relatively normal [purves2018].
