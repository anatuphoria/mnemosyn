## Function
The twelve thoracic segments of the spinal cord, serving the chest wall, the abdominal wall and the muscles between the ribs. Together with the first lumbar segments they hold the sympathetic neurons for the whole body [purves2018].

## Connections
Sympathetic neurons in the lateral horn of these segments send their axons through the ventral roots to the sympathetic chain [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Injury at the thoracic level spares the arms but paralyses the legs (paraplegia) [purves2018].
