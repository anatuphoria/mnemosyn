## Function
The fourth thoracic segment. It carries sensation from the band of chest at the level of the nipples [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres form the fourth intercostal nerve; its lateral horn holds sympathetic neurons [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The nipple line is a useful landmark for the T4 sensory level [purves2018].
