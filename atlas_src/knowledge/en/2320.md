## Function
Lobule III of the anterior lobe of the cerebellum. The anterior lobe holds a map of the body and coordinates movement of the trunk and limbs [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the spinal cord through the spinocerebellar tracts and projects through the interposed and fastigial nuclei [kandel2021].
