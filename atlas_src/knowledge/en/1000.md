## Function
Contains the primary motor cortex, which sends the commands for voluntary movement of the opposite side of the body [purves2018]. Body parts are represented in order along the gyrus, with the face and hand occupying a large share of it [penfield1937].

## Connections
Receives input from premotor cortex, the supplementary motor area, primary somatosensory cortex and the ventral lateral nucleus of the thalamus, which relays output of the cerebellum and basal ganglia [kandel2021]. Its output descends in the corticospinal and corticobulbar tracts to the spinal cord and brainstem motor nuclei [purves2018].

## Clinical
Damage causes weakness of the opposite side of the body with upper motor neuron signs, such as spasticity and increased reflexes that develop over time [purves2018].
