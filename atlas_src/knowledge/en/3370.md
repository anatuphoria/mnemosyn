## Function
The nerve that carries vision from the retina. It is a tract of the central nervous system, made of the axons of retinal ganglion cells [purves2018].

## Connections
It leaves the eye, passes through the optic canal and joins the other optic nerve at the optic chiasm, where fibres from the inner half of each retina cross [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of vision in that eye and a weak pupil response to light shone in it; inflammation of the nerve (optic neuritis) is common in multiple sclerosis [purves2018].
