## Function
The lobe hidden deep in the lateral sulcus, covered by the opercula of the frontal, parietal and temporal lobes. It holds primary taste cortex and maps the state of the body, such as heart beat, breathing and pain, and it takes part in emotion and disgust [purves2018] [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the thalamus, including the taste and visceral relays, and is linked with the amygdala, cingulate cortex and orbitofrontal cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It is often damaged in strokes of the middle cerebral artery [purves2018].
