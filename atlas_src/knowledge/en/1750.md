## Function
The medial part of the parietal lobe, in front of the occipital lobe. It takes part in recalling episodes from one's own past, imagining scenes and mental imagery [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the posterior cingulate cortex, the prefrontal cortex and the hippocampal region, and with the pulvinar and lateral posterior thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It is among the first regions to show reduced metabolism in Alzheimer disease [kandel2021].
