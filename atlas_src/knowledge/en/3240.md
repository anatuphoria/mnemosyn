## Function
The pathway for fine touch, vibration and position sense from the opposite side of the body, between the medulla and the thalamus [purves2018].

## Connections
It starts in the gracile and cuneate nuclei of the medulla, crosses as the internal arcuate fibres, ascends through the brainstem and ends in the ventral posterolateral nucleus of the thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage above the medulla causes loss of fine touch and position sense on the opposite side [purves2018].
