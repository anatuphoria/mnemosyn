## Function
The largest cerebral artery. It supplies most of the lateral surface of the hemisphere, including the motor and sensory areas for the face and arm and, in the dominant hemisphere, the language areas, and through deep branches the basal nuclei and internal capsule [purves2018].

## Connections
It arises from the internal carotid artery, gives off the lateral lenticulostriate arteries, and runs in the lateral sulcus over the insula before spreading over the cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A blockage of its stem causes weakness and sensory loss of the opposite face and arm more than the leg, loss of the opposite visual field, and aphasia or neglect depending on the side [purves2018].
