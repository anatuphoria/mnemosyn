## Function
A long bundle that joins the occipital and temporal cortex with the frontal lobe. It is thought to take part in reading, the meaning of words and visual attention [kandel2021].

## Connections
It runs from the occipital lobe through the extreme and external capsules to the prefrontal and orbitofrontal cortex [kandel2021].
