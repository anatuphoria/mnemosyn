## Function
The sixth thoracic segment. It carries sensation from a band of the lower chest near the bottom of the breastbone [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres form the sixth intercostal nerve; its lateral horn holds sympathetic neurons, including some for the abdominal organs [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Injury above about T6 can lead to autonomic dysreflexia, a dangerous rise in blood pressure triggered by stimuli below the injury [kandel2021].
