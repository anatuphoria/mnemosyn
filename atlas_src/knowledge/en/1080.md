## Function
The olfactory cortex receives smell information directly from the olfactory bulb, without a relay in the thalamus. Smell is the only sense that reaches the cortex this way [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the lateral olfactory tract and sends output to the orbitofrontal cortex, the amygdala, the entorhinal cortex and the hypothalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Loss of the sense of smell (anosmia) is an early sign in some neurodegenerative diseases and follows head injury that tears the olfactory fibres [purves2018].
