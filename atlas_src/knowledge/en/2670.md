## Function
A band of cholinergic neurons in the basal forebrain, running from the septum down to the amygdala region. Its vertical limb supplies acetylcholine to the hippocampus, which supports the theta rhythm and memory [hasselmo2006].

## Connections
It projects to the hippocampus through the fornix and to the olfactory bulb and cortex [kandel2021].
