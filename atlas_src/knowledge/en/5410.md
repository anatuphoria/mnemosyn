## Function
The middle part of the spinal grey matter. It holds interneurons that coordinate movement and, in the thoracic and upper lumbar segments, the sympathetic neurons of the lateral horn and Clarke's column [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the descending tracts and sensory fibres and sends signals to the motor neurons, the sympathetic chain and the cerebellum [kandel2021].
