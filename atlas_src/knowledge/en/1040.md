## Function
The middle part of the inferior frontal gyrus. In the dominant hemisphere it is the other part of Broca's area and takes part in speech production and in processing the meaning of words [purves2018].

## Connections
It is linked with the temporal lobe through the arcuate fasciculus and through the extreme capsule [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage in the dominant hemisphere contributes to Broca's aphasia [purves2018].
