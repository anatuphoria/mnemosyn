## Function
Contains dopamine-producing neurons whose projections to the striatum support the initiation and selection of movement through the basal ganglia circuits [purves2018].

## Connections
Sends dopaminergic axons to the caudate nucleus and putamen (the nigrostriatal pathway) and receives input from the striatum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Degeneration of these dopaminergic neurons underlies Parkinson disease, with slowness of movement, rigidity and resting tremor [kandel2021].
