## Function
The part of the ventral striatum where the caudate and putamen meet below the anterior limb of the internal capsule. It takes part in reward, motivation and learning from rewards [schultz1997].

## Connections
It receives dopamine from the ventral tegmental area and input from the prefrontal cortex, amygdala and hippocampus, and projects to the ventral pallidum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It is central to the effects of addictive drugs, which all raise dopamine in it [kandel2021].
