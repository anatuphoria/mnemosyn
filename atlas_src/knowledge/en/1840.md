## Function
The thalamic relay for touch, position sense, pain and temperature from the opposite side of the body below the head [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the medial lemniscus and the spinothalamic tract and projects to the primary somatosensory cortex in the postcentral gyrus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A small stroke here can cause loss of all sensation on the opposite side of the body (pure sensory stroke) [purves2018].
