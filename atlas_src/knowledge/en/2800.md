## Function
The first station of the hearing pathway in the brain. Fibres of the cochlear nerve end here, keeping the frequency map of the cochlea [purves2018].

## Connections
They receive the auditory part of the vestibulocochlear nerve and project to the superior olive of both sides and to the inferior colliculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage here or to the cochlear nerve causes deafness in the ear of the same side [purves2018].
