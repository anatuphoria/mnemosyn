## Function
The back part of the cingulate gyrus. It takes part in recalling one's own past, orientation in space and the brain's activity at rest [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the precuneus, the hippocampal region and the anterior thalamus; the cingulum runs beneath it [papez1937] [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It shows reduced metabolism early in Alzheimer disease [kandel2021].
