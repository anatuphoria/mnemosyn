## Function
Groups of neurons along the midline of the brainstem that make serotonin. They influence mood, sleep, appetite and pain [berger2009].

## Connections
The upper raphe nuclei project to the forebrain, and the lower ones to the brainstem and spinal cord [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Many antidepressant drugs act by increasing serotonin released from these neurons [berger2009].
