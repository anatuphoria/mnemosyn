## Function
The five lumbar segments of the spinal cord, serving the front and sides of the legs and the muscles of the hip, knee and ankle [purves2018].

## Connections
They lie behind the lowest thoracic and first lumbar vertebrae; their roots run down in the cauda equina and form the lumbar plexus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Pressure on the lumbar roots from a slipped disc causes pain and weakness down the leg (sciatica for the lower roots) [purves2018].
