## Function
The sixth cervical segment. It carries sensation from the outer forearm and thumb and supplies the muscles that extend the wrist and bend the elbow [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the upper trunk of the brachial plexus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The brachioradialis reflex tests mainly C6 [purves2018].
