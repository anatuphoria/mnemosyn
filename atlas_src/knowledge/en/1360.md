## Function
The gyrus on the medial underside of the temporal lobe, next to the hippocampus. Its front part holds the entorhinal cortex, the main gateway for information entering and leaving the hippocampus; its back part takes part in memory for scenes and places [squire2004].

## Connections
It receives from widespread association cortex and projects to the hippocampus through the perforant path [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The entorhinal cortex is one of the first places affected by the neurofibrillary changes of Alzheimer disease [braak1991].
