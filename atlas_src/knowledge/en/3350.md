## Function
The pathway that carries vision from the lateral geniculate nucleus to the primary visual cortex [purves2018].

## Connections
Its lower fibres loop forward in the temporal lobe (Meyer's loop) before turning back; its upper fibres run through the parietal lobe; all end in the calcarine cortex [purves2018].

## Clinical
Damage to the temporal loop causes loss of the upper quarter of the opposite visual field, and damage to the parietal fibres the lower quarter [purves2018].
