## Function
The inferior parietal lobule, below the intraparietal sulcus, made of the supramarginal and angular gyri. It takes part in spatial attention and, in the dominant hemisphere, in language and calculation [purves2018].

## Connections
It is linked with the frontal lobe through the superior longitudinal fasciculus and the arcuate fasciculus, and with the temporal lobe [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Right sided damage often causes neglect of the left side of space [purves2018].
