## Function
Makes melatonin, mostly at night, under the control of the light-dark cycle, and so helps set the daily sleep-wake rhythm [purves2018].

## Connections
A small gland on the roof of the third ventricle, behind the habenula and above the superior colliculi. Light information reaches it from the retina through the suprachiasmatic nucleus of the hypothalamus and a sympathetic pathway through the superior cervical ganglion [purves2018].

## Clinical
Tumours of the pineal region can press on the dorsal midbrain and cause Parinaud syndrome, with loss of upward gaze, and can block the cerebral aqueduct and cause hydrocephalus. The gland often calcifies with age, which shows on CT scans [kandel2021].
