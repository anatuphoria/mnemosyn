## Function
The gyrus below the calcarine sulcus on the medial occipital surface. Its primary visual cortex maps the upper quarter of the opposite visual field, and further forward it takes part in colour vision [purves2018] [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives the lower part of the optic radiation, which loops through the temporal lobe (Meyer's loop) [purves2018].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of the upper quarter of the opposite visual field; damage further forward can cause loss of colour vision (achromatopsia) [kandel2021].
