## Function
The primary somatosensory cortex for touch, vibration, position and pain of the opposite side of the body. Body parts are represented in order along the gyrus, as in the motor map in front of it [penfield1937].

## Connections
It receives from the ventral posterolateral and ventral posteromedial nuclei of the thalamus and projects to the parietal association cortex and the motor cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of fine touch, position sense and the ability to recognise objects by touch on the opposite side [purves2018].
