## Function
The main output tract of the cerebellum. It carries signals from the dentate and interposed nuclei to the red nucleus and thalamus [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres cross in the midbrain and end in the opposite red nucleus and ventral lateral thalamus; it also carries the ventral spinocerebellar tract into the cerebellum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage before it crosses causes intention tremor and clumsiness of the limbs on the same side; damage after the crossing affects the opposite side [purves2018].
