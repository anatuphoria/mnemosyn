## Function
The eight cervical segments of the spinal cord, serving the back of the head, the neck, the arms and the diaphragm [purves2018].

## Connections
The segments C5 to T1 form the brachial plexus to the arm; C3 to C5 form the phrenic nerve to the diaphragm [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Injury at the upper cervical level can stop breathing and paralyse all four limbs [purves2018].
