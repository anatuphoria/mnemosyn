## Function
The nerve for hearing and balance, made of the cochlear and vestibular nerves [purves2018].

## Connections
It leaves the inner ear through the internal acoustic meatus and enters the brainstem at the junction of the pons and medulla, ending in the cochlear and vestibular nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A benign tumour of its sheath (vestibular schwannoma) causes slowly worsening hearing loss and ringing in one ear [purves2018].
