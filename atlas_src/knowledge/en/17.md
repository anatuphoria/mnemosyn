## Function
The ring of cortex on the medial side of each hemisphere around the corpus callosum and brainstem: the cingulate gyrus, the parahippocampal gyrus and the hippocampal formation. It takes part in emotion, motivation and memory [purves2018].

## Connections
Its parts are joined in a loop with the mammillary bodies and anterior thalamus, described by Papez, and are linked with the amygdala, hypothalamus and prefrontal cortex [papez1937].
