## Function
The thalamic relay for vision: it receives retinal input and passes it to the primary visual cortex [purves2018].

## Connections
Receives axons of retinal ganglion cells from both eyes through the optic tract, with the input from each eye kept in separate layers, and projects through the optic radiation to the primary visual cortex around the calcarine sulcus [purves2018].

## Clinical
Damage to one lateral geniculate nucleus causes loss of vision in the opposite visual field of both eyes [kandel2021].
