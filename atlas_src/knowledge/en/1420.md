## Function
The outer part of the lentiform nucleus. With the caudate it forms the striatum; the putamen is the main entry point of the basal ganglia motor loop [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the motor and somatosensory cortex and from dopamine neurons of the substantia nigra pars compacta, and projects to both parts of the globus pallidus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Loss of its dopamine input causes the slowness and rigidity of Parkinson disease; it is also a common site of hypertensive haemorrhage [purves2018].
