## Function
The artery that supplies the occipital lobe, the underside and medial side of the temporal lobe, and through deep branches the thalamus and upper midbrain [purves2018].

## Connections
The two posterior cerebral arteries arise from the top of the basilar artery and are joined to the carotid circulation by the posterior communicating arteries [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A blockage causes loss of the opposite visual field, often sparing central vision, and can cause memory problems or thalamic sensory loss [purves2018].
