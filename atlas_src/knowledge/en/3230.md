## Function
A small commissure at the junction of the midbrain and diencephalon that takes part in the pupillary light reflex and vertical eye movements [purves2018].

## Connections
It crosses above the opening of the cerebral aqueduct and carries fibres from the pretectal nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Pressure on the dorsal midbrain near it, for example from a pineal tumour, can cause loss of upward gaze (Parinaud syndrome) [purves2018].
