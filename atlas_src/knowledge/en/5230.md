## Function
The third lumbar segment. It carries sensation from the front of the thigh down to the inner knee and supplies the quadriceps, which straightens the knee [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the femoral and obturator nerves [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The knee reflex tests segments L3 and L4 [purves2018].
