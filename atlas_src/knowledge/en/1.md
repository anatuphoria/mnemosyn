## Function
The brain and spinal cord together. It receives information from the senses and the body, integrates it, and sends out the commands for movement, glands and organs [purves2018].

## Connections
It links to the rest of the body through the cranial nerves, which leave the brain, and the spinal nerves, which leave the spinal cord; together these make up the peripheral nervous system [kandel2021].
