## Function
A brainstem tract that carries descending fibres from the red nucleus to the inferior olive and ascending fibres for taste and arousal [kandel2021].

## Connections
It runs from the midbrain through the pontine tegmentum to the inferior olive [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to it can cause palatal tremor through changes in the inferior olive [kandel2021].
