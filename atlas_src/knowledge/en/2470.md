## Function
Lobule X of the vermis, the nodulus. With the flocculus it forms the vestibulocerebellum, which takes part in balance and in steadying the eyes [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the vestibular nerve and nuclei and projects back to the vestibular nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes unsteadiness when standing and walking [purves2018].
