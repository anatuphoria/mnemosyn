## Function
The temporal cortex supplied by the middle cerebral artery: the superior and middle temporal gyri, including Wernicke's area in the dominant hemisphere [purves2018].

## Connections
Its branches come mainly from the lower division of the middle cerebral artery [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct here in the dominant hemisphere causes Wernicke's aphasia, often with loss of the upper quarter of the opposite visual field [purves2018].
