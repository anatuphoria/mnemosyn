## Function
The largest division of the brain, made of the telencephalon (the cerebral hemispheres) and the diencephalon (thalamus, hypothalamus and their neighbours). It holds the circuits for perception, voluntary movement, language, memory and emotion [purves2018].

## Connections
It develops from the most rostral of the three primary brain vesicles and joins the brainstem at the midbrain [kandel2021].
