## Function
The vertebral and basilar arteries and their branches, which supply the brainstem, the cerebellum and, through the posterior cerebral arteries, the occipital lobes and thalamus [purves2018].

## Connections
The two vertebral arteries enter the skull through the foramen magnum and join to form the basilar artery in front of the pons [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Strokes here can cause dizziness, double vision, trouble swallowing, clumsiness and weakness or sensory loss on both sides [purves2018].
