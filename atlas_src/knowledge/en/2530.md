## Function
A thin layer of neurons along the wall of the third ventricle. It releases hormones that control the anterior pituitary, including somatostatin, which inhibits growth hormone release [kandel2021].

## Connections
Its neurons send axons to the median eminence, where the hormones enter the portal veins to the pituitary [kandel2021].
