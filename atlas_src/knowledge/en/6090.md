## Function
The occipital cortex supplied by the posterior cerebral artery, including the primary visual cortex around the calcarine sulcus [purves2018].

## Connections
Its branches, including the calcarine artery, run along the medial occipital surface [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct causes loss of the opposite half of the visual field in both eyes; when it is in the dominant hemisphere and includes the splenium, a person may lose reading but still write (alexia without agraphia) [purves2018].
