## Function
The upper back part of the diencephalon, made of the pineal gland, the habenula and the posterior commissure region. The pineal gland releases melatonin at night under the control of the circadian clock [saper2005].

## Connections
The pineal gland receives the clock signal from the suprachiasmatic nucleus through a sympathetic pathway; the habenula links the forebrain with midbrain dopamine and serotonin neurons [kandel2021].
