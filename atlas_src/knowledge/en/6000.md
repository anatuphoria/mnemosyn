## Function
The cortex and white matter supplied by the anterior cerebral artery: the medial frontal and parietal lobes, including the paracentral lobule, and most of the corpus callosum [purves2018].

## Connections
Its branches run along the medial surface of the hemisphere above the corpus callosum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct here causes weakness and sensory loss of the opposite leg more than the arm, and can cause reduced drive and incontinence [purves2018].
