## Function
The two lateral parts of the cerebellum. They take part in planning and coordinating skilled limb movements and in timing [purves2018].

## Connections
They receive from the cortex through the pontine nuclei and project through the dentate nucleus to the opposite ventral lateral thalamus and motor cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes clumsiness and tremor during movement of the limbs on the same side [purves2018].
