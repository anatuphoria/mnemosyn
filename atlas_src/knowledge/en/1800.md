## Function
One of the anterior nuclei of the thalamus. It is a station in the Papez circuit and takes part in memory and spatial orientation [papez1937].

## Connections
It receives the mammillothalamic tract from the mammillary body and fibres from the hippocampus, and projects to the cingulate cortex [kandel2021].
