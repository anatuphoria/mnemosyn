## Function
The largest deep cerebellar nucleus. It carries output of the cerebellar hemispheres for planning skilled movements and for cognition [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the lateral cerebellar hemisphere and projects through the superior cerebellar peduncle to the opposite ventral lateral thalamus and red nucleus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to it or its output tract causes tremor during movement (intention tremor) of the limbs on the same side [purves2018].
