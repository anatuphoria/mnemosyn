## Function
The back part of the inferior parietal lobule, around the end of the superior temporal sulcus. In the dominant hemisphere it takes part in reading, writing and calculation [purves2018].

## Connections
It is linked with the visual association cortex, the temporal language areas and the frontal lobe [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage in the dominant hemisphere can cause Gerstmann syndrome: trouble with writing, calculation, naming fingers and telling left from right [kandel2021].
