## Function
The back part of the inferior frontal gyrus. In the dominant hemisphere, usually the left, it forms part of Broca's area, which is needed for producing fluent speech and grammar [purves2018].

## Connections
It is joined to the temporal and parietal language areas by the arcuate fasciculus and the superior longitudinal fasciculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage in the dominant hemisphere causes Broca's (expressive) aphasia: slow, effortful speech with relatively spared understanding [purves2018].
