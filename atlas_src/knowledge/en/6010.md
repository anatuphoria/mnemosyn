## Function
The deep region supplied by the medial lenticulostriate arteries and the recurrent artery of Heubner: the head of the caudate, the front of the putamen and globus pallidus, and the anterior limb of the internal capsule [kandel2021].

## Connections
These small arteries arise from the first part of the anterior cerebral artery and enter the brain through the anterior perforated substance [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Small deep infarcts here (lacunes) can cause weakness of the face and arm or changes in behaviour [kandel2021].
