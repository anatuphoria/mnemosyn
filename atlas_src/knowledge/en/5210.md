## Function
The first lumbar segment. It carries sensation from the groin and contributes to bending the hip; like the thoracic segments it holds sympathetic neurons, which reach down to L2 [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres form the iliohypogastric, ilioinguinal and genitofemoral nerves [kandel2021].
