## Function
The fourth lumbar segment. It carries sensation from the inner lower leg and supplies the quadriceps and the muscle that lifts the foot [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the femoral nerve and the lumbosacral trunk [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The knee reflex tests segments L3 and L4 [purves2018].
