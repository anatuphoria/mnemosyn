## Function
A group of nuclei in the medulla and pons that receives balance information from the inner ear. They keep the eyes steady when the head moves and help keep the body upright [purves2018].

## Connections
They receive from the vestibular nerve and cerebellum and project to the eye muscle nuclei through the medial longitudinal fasciculus, to the spinal cord through the vestibulospinal tracts, and to the cerebellum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes vertigo, nystagmus and unsteadiness [purves2018].
