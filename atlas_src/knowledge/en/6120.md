## Function
The brainstem supplied by the basilar artery: mainly the pons and midbrain through its paramedian and short branches [purves2018].

## Connections
Its paramedian branches enter the front of the pons and midbrain near the midline [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A blockage of the basilar artery can cause locked in syndrome or coma [purves2018].
