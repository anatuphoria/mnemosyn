## Function
Lobules IV and V of the vermis, part of the anterior lobe. They take part in control of the trunk and walking [kandel2021].

## Connections
They receive from the spinocerebellar tracts and project through the fastigial nucleus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The anterior vermis shrinks in chronic alcohol use, causing an unsteady gait [purves2018].
