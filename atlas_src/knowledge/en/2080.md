## Function
The upper pair of bumps on the back of the midbrain. It turns the eyes and head towards things that are seen, heard or felt, and drives fast eye movements (saccades) [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the retina, the visual cortex and the frontal eye field and projects to the brainstem gaze centres, the pulvinar and the spinal cord through the tectospinal tract [kandel2021].
