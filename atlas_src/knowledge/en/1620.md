## Function
The upper gyrus of the lateral temporal lobe. It holds auditory association cortex, and its back part in the dominant hemisphere forms Wernicke's area for understanding speech [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the primary auditory cortex and is linked with Broca's area through the arcuate fasciculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the back part in the dominant hemisphere causes Wernicke (receptive) aphasia: fluent speech with errors and poor understanding [purves2018].
