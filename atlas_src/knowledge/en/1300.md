## Function
The part of the anterior cingulate cortex below the genu of the corpus callosum. It takes part in mood and in control of the autonomic and stress responses [ulrichlai2009].

## Connections
It is linked with the amygdala, hypothalamus and brainstem nuclei such as the periaqueductal grey [ulrichlai2009].

## Clinical
Changes in its activity have been reported in major depression, and it has been a target for deep brain stimulation in treatment resistant depression [kandel2021].
