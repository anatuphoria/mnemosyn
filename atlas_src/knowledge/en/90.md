## Function
The regions of brain supplied by each major artery. Knowing them lets a doctor tell from a person's symptoms which artery is blocked in a stroke [purves2018].

## Connections
The anterior and middle cerebral arteries come from the internal carotid artery; the posterior cerebral arteries come from the basilar artery, which the two vertebral arteries form; the circle of Willis joins them at the base of the brain [kandel2021].
