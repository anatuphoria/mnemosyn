## Function
The wedge of occipital cortex above the calcarine sulcus. Its primary visual cortex maps the lower quarter of the opposite visual field [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the upper part of the optic radiation, which runs through the parietal lobe [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of the lower quarter of the opposite visual field in both eyes [purves2018].
