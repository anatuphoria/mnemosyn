## Function
The gyrus between the superior and inferior frontal sulci. It belongs to the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex and holds the frontal eye field at its back, which moves both eyes towards the opposite side [purves2018] [miller2001].

## Connections
It is linked with the parietal cortex through the superior longitudinal fasciculus, with the mediodorsal nucleus of the thalamus, and with the superior colliculus for eye movements [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An acute lesion near the frontal eye field can turn both eyes towards the side of the lesion [purves2018].
