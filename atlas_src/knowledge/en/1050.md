## Function
The front part of the inferior frontal gyrus, where it turns onto the underside of the frontal lobe. It joins the orbitofrontal cortex in judging the value of outcomes and in language processing [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is joined to the temporal pole and amygdala by the uncinate fasciculus [kandel2021].
