## Function
The straight gyrus on the underside of the frontal lobe, next to the midline and the olfactory tract. It belongs to the ventromedial prefrontal and orbitofrontal cortex [kandel2021].

## Connections
It lies beside the olfactory tract and is linked with the other orbitofrontal gyri, the amygdala and the hypothalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It is often injured in frontal head trauma and by bleeding from an aneurysm of the anterior communicating artery [purves2018].
