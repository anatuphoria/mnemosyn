## Function
A nucleus above the optic tract whose large neurons make vasopressin and oxytocin. Vasopressin release rises when the blood becomes too concentrated, which lets the kidney keep water [robertson1976] [purves2018].

## Connections
Its axons run down the pituitary stalk and release their hormones from the posterior pituitary [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Loss of these neurons or their axons causes central diabetes insipidus, with large volumes of dilute urine [christcrain2019].
