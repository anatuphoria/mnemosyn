## Function
A group of neurons below the locus coeruleus that is active in rapid eye movement sleep and stops the muscles from moving during dreams [saper2005].

## Connections
It projects to the medulla and spinal cord to inhibit motor neurons [saper2005].

## Clinical
Damage in this region is thought to cause rapid eye movement sleep behaviour disorder, in which people act out their dreams [kandel2021].
