## Function
The upper part of the temporal pole, the front tip of the temporal lobe. It takes part in linking objects and people with their meaning and emotional value [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is joined to the orbitofrontal cortex by the uncinate fasciculus and is linked with the amygdala [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The temporal pole is affected early in the semantic variant of frontotemporal dementia, with loss of word meaning [kandel2021].
