## Function
The reticular formation of the midbrain, part of the ascending arousal system that keeps the cortex awake [purves2018].

## Connections
It projects to the thalamus, hypothalamus and basal forebrain and receives from many sensory pathways [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the arousal system in the upper brainstem on both sides can cause coma [purves2018].
