## Function
The longest cranial nerve. It carries parasympathetic commands to the heart, lungs and gut, sensation from these organs, and commands to the muscles of the throat and voice box [purves2018].

## Connections
It leaves the side of the medulla; its fibres come from the dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus and the nucleus ambiguus, and its sensory fibres end in the nucleus of the solitary tract [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes hoarseness and trouble swallowing, with the soft palate pulled towards the healthy side [purves2018].
