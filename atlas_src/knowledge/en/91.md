## Function
The artery that supplies the medial surface of the frontal and parietal lobes, including the leg and foot areas of the motor and sensory cortex, and through deep branches the front of the basal nuclei [purves2018].

## Connections
It arises from the internal carotid artery, is joined to its partner by the anterior communicating artery, and runs above the corpus callosum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A blockage causes weakness and sensory loss mainly in the opposite leg, and can cause apathy and loss of bladder control [purves2018].
