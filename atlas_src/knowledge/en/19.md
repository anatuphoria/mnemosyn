## Function
The structures on the underside of the forebrain below the basal nuclei, including the nucleus basalis of Meynert and the diagonal band of Broca. Their cholinergic neurons supply acetylcholine to the cortex and hippocampus [hasselmo2006].

## Connections
They project widely to the cerebral cortex, the hippocampus and the amygdala [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The cholinergic neurons of the basal forebrain degenerate in Alzheimer disease [hasselmo2006].
