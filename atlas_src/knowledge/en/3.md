## Function
The part of the central nervous system inside the vertebral canal. It carries sensation from the body up to the brain and commands down to the muscles, and holds the circuits for reflexes and rhythmic movements such as walking [purves2018].

## Connections
It continues from the medulla at the foramen magnum and ends near the first or second lumbar vertebra; 31 pairs of spinal nerves leave it through dorsal and ventral roots [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of movement and sensation below the level of the lesion, and often loss of bladder and bowel control [purves2018].
