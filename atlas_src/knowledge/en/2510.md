## Function
A nucleus next to the third ventricle that leads the stress response. Its small neurons release corticotropin releasing hormone (CRH) to the pituitary, its large neurons make vasopressin and oxytocin for the posterior pituitary, and other neurons drive the autonomic nervous system [ulrichlai2009].

## Connections
It receives from the brainstem, the bed nucleus of the stria terminalis and the limbic system, and projects to the median eminence, the posterior pituitary and the brainstem and spinal autonomic centres [ulrichlai2009].

## Clinical
Its output is reduced by cortisol through negative feedback, which a dexamethasone suppression test makes use of [nieman2008].
