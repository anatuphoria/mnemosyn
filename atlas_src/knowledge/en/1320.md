## Function
The part of the anterior cingulate cortex above the corpus callosum. It takes part in noticing conflict and errors, in effortful attention, and in the unpleasant feeling of pain [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, the motor areas and the medial thalamus [kandel2021].
