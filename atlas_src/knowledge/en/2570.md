## Function
A nucleus at the base of the hypothalamus that senses the body's energy stores. It reads hormones such as leptin, insulin and ghrelin and drives hunger or fullness, and it controls growth hormone and prolactin [morton2006] [cummings2001].

## Connections
Its neurons project to the paraventricular nucleus and lateral hypothalamus and to the median eminence [morton2006].
