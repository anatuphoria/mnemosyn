## Function
The deep region supplied by the lateral lenticulostriate arteries: most of the putamen, the outer globus pallidus, the body of the caudate and the upper part of the internal capsule [kandel2021].

## Connections
These small arteries arise from the first part of the middle cerebral artery and enter the brain through the anterior perforated substance [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Small infarcts here are a common cause of pure motor stroke; these vessels are the most common source of hypertensive haemorrhage in the putamen [purves2018].
