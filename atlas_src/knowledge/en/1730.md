## Function
The front part of the inferior parietal lobule, curving around the end of the lateral sulcus. In the dominant hemisphere it takes part in the sound and word processing of language; it also contributes to spatial attention and skilled movement [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with Broca's area through the arcuate fasciculus and the superior longitudinal fasciculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage in the dominant hemisphere can cause conduction aphasia or ideomotor apraxia [purves2018].
