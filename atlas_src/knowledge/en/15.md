## Function
The lower lateral part of each hemisphere, below the lateral sulcus. It holds the primary auditory cortex, language comprehension areas in the dominant hemisphere, areas for recognising objects and faces, and on its medial side the hippocampus and amygdala for memory and emotion [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives auditory input from the medial geniculate nucleus and visual input from the occipital lobe, and is linked with the frontal lobe through the uncinate and arcuate fasciculi [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage can cause Wernicke aphasia, loss of memory for new events, or failure to recognise faces; the medial temporal lobe is a common site where focal seizures begin [purves2018].
