## Function
The front part of the cingulate gyrus, around the genu of the corpus callosum. It takes part in emotion, the feeling of pain as unpleasant, motivation and noticing errors and conflict [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the prefrontal cortex, amygdala, hypothalamus and insula, and with the brainstem centres that control the internal organs [kandel2021].
