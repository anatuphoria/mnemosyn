## Function
The medial magnocellular part of the mediodorsal nucleus. It links the amygdala and olfactory regions with the orbitofrontal cortex and takes part in emotion and reward guided decisions [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the amygdala, olfactory cortex and ventral pallidum and projects to the orbital and medial prefrontal cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the mediodorsal nucleus contributes to memory loss in thalamic strokes and in Korsakoff syndrome [kandel2021].
