## Function
The bundles of nerve fibres (white matter) that carry signals between grey matter regions. They are grouped by where they run: projection, association and commissural tracts, brainstem tracts, cerebellar peduncles, limbic tracts, radiations and cranial nerves [purves2018].

## Connections
Their fibres are axons wrapped in myelin, which makes conduction fast and gives white matter its colour [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to white matter, from stroke, injury or demyelinating disease such as multiple sclerosis, disconnects the regions it joins [purves2018].
