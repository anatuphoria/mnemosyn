## Function
The back of each hemisphere, devoted to vision. It holds the primary visual cortex around the calcarine sulcus and visual association areas around it [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the optic radiation from the lateral geniculate nucleus and sends visual information forward in a dorsal stream to the parietal lobe and a ventral stream to the temporal lobe [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to one occipital lobe causes loss of the opposite half of the visual field in both eyes (homonymous hemianopia) [purves2018].
