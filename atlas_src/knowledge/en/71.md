## Function
Tracts that join the cortex with lower parts of the nervous system, in both directions: the thalamus, basal nuclei, brainstem and spinal cord [purves2018].

## Connections
Most of them pass through the internal capsule and fan out above it as the corona radiata [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A small stroke in the internal capsule, where these fibres are packed tightly, can cause weakness of the whole opposite side [purves2018].
