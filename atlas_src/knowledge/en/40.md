## Function
The large structure behind the brainstem that makes movements smooth, accurate and well timed. It compares intended with actual movement and corrects errors, learns motor skills, and takes part in balance and some cognitive functions [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the cortex through the pontine nuclei, from the spinal cord and vestibular system, and climbing fibres from the inferior olive; it sends output through the deep cerebellar nuclei to the thalamus and brainstem [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes clumsiness of the limbs on the same side, an unsteady broad based gait, slurred speech and nystagmus [purves2018].
