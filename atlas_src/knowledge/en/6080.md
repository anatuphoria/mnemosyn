## Function
The temporal cortex supplied by the posterior cerebral artery: the underside and medial side of the temporal lobe, including the parahippocampal and fusiform gyri and part of the hippocampus [purves2018].

## Connections
Its branches run along the underside of the temporal lobe [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct here can cause memory loss, especially when both sides are affected, and difficulty recognising faces [kandel2021].
