## Function
The only source of histamine in the brain. Its neurons fire during waking and promote arousal [saper2005].

## Connections
It projects to almost the whole brain and is inhibited during sleep by the ventrolateral preoptic nucleus [saper2005].

## Clinical
Antihistamines that enter the brain cause drowsiness by blocking its effect [saper2005].
