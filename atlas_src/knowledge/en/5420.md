## Function
The back part of the spinal grey matter, where sensory fibres from the body end. It processes touch, pain and temperature and can dampen pain signals before they rise to the brain [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the dorsal root fibres and sends axons into the spinothalamic tract and other ascending pathways; it receives descending control from the brainstem [kandel2021].
