## Function
The midbrain, pons and medulla, which join the forebrain to the spinal cord. It holds most cranial nerve nuclei, the centres for breathing, heart rate and blood pressure, and the networks that keep a person awake [purves2018].

## Connections
All the long ascending and descending tracts between the brain and spinal cord pass through it, and it is joined to the cerebellum by the three cerebellar peduncles [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Brainstem strokes often combine a cranial nerve palsy on one side with weakness or sensory loss of the limbs on the other side; large lesions can cause coma or stop breathing [purves2018].
