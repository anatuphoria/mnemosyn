## Function
The lateral part of the superior frontal gyrus. Together with the middle frontal gyrus it forms much of the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, which is engaged in working memory, planning and the control of attention [miller2001].

## Connections
It exchanges fibres with the parietal association cortex through the superior longitudinal fasciculus and with the mediodorsal nucleus of the thalamus [kandel2021].
