## Function
A nucleus in the middle of the hypothalamus. It takes part in energy balance and blood glucose control, and in defensive behaviour [morton2006].

## Connections
It receives from the arcuate nucleus and amygdala and projects to other hypothalamic nuclei and the brainstem [morton2006].

## Clinical
In experimental animals, damage to it causes overeating and obesity [morton2006].
