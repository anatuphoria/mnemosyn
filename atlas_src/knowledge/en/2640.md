## Function
A small lens shaped nucleus that is part of the basal ganglia. It excites the internal globus pallidus and helps stop or hold back movements [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the external globus pallidus and the motor cortex and projects to both segments of the globus pallidus and the substantia nigra pars reticulata [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes violent flinging movements of the opposite limbs (hemiballismus); it is the most common target for deep brain stimulation in Parkinson disease [purves2018].
