## Function
The transverse temporal gyrus on the upper surface of the temporal lobe, hidden in the lateral sulcus. It holds the primary auditory cortex, with frequencies mapped in order along it [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the acoustic radiation from the medial geniculate nucleus and projects to the surrounding auditory association cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Because each ear reaches both sides, damage on one side causes little deafness but impairs locating sounds; damage on both sides can cause cortical deafness [purves2018].
