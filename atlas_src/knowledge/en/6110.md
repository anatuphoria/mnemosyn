## Function
The deep region supplied by the anterior choroidal artery and nearby thalamoperforating branches: the lower part of the posterior limb of the internal capsule, the optic tract, the inner globus pallidus and parts of the thalamus and medial temporal lobe [kandel2021].

## Connections
The anterior choroidal artery arises from the internal carotid artery and runs back along the optic tract [kandel2021]. In this atlas the territory is listed with the posterior cerebral artery territories.

## Clinical
An infarct can cause weakness, sensory loss and loss of the opposite visual field together [kandel2021].
