## Function
The gyrus on the underside of the temporal and occipital lobes, between the parahippocampal and inferior temporal gyri. It holds areas specialised for recognising faces and, in the left hemisphere, written words [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the visual cortex through the ventral visual stream and the inferior longitudinal fasciculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage on both sides or on the right can cause loss of face recognition (prosopagnosia); damage on the left can impair reading [kandel2021].
