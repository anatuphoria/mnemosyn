## Function
The fifth lumbar segment. It carries sensation from the outer lower leg and the top of the foot and supplies the muscles that lift the foot and the big toe [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the lumbosacral trunk and the sciatic nerve [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the L5 root, often from a slipped disc, causes foot drop and numbness on the top of the foot [purves2018].
