## Function
The fifth sacral segment, near the tip of the conus medullaris. It carries sensation from the skin right around the anus [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the coccygeal plexus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Testing sensation around the anus checks whether a spinal cord injury is complete [purves2018].
