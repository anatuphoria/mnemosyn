## Function
A long bundle that joins the occipital lobe with the front of the temporal lobe. It carries the ventral visual stream for recognising objects and faces [kandel2021].

## Connections
It runs along the lateral wall of the temporal horn of the lateral ventricle from the occipital cortex to the temporal pole and amygdala [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage can impair recognition of faces or objects [kandel2021].
