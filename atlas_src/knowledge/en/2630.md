## Function
A pair of round nuclei on the underside of the hypothalamus. They are a station in the Papez circuit and take part in memory [papez1937].

## Connections
They receive the fornix from the hippocampus and project to the anterior thalamus through the mammillothalamic tract [kandel2021].

## Clinical
They are damaged in Wernicke encephalopathy and Korsakoff syndrome from thiamine deficiency, which causes severe memory loss [kandel2021].
