## Function
The nerve that moves the muscles of facial expression and carries taste from the front of the tongue; it also controls tears and saliva [purves2018].

## Connections
It leaves the brainstem at the junction of the pons and medulla, crosses the internal acoustic meatus and runs through the temporal bone to the face [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A palsy of the nerve (as in Bell's palsy) weakens the whole face on one side, while damage above the facial nucleus mainly spares the forehead [purves2018].
