## Function
The nuclei inside the cerebellar white matter that carry all of its output: the dentate, the interposed (emboliform and globose) and the fastigial nuclei [purves2018].

## Connections
They receive from Purkinje cells of the cerebellar cortex and from the fibres entering the cerebellum, and project to the thalamus, red nucleus, vestibular nuclei and reticular formation [kandel2021].
