## Function
Groups of nuclei inside the internal medullary lamina of the thalamus, including the centromedian nucleus. They take part in arousal, attention and the response to pain [kandel2021].

## Connections
They receive from the brainstem reticular formation, the spinothalamic tract and the basal ganglia, and project widely to the cortex and to the striatum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage on both sides, as when one thalamoperforating artery supplies both sides, can cause deep drowsiness or coma [purves2018].
