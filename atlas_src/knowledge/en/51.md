## Function
A band of grey matter in the midbrain made of the pars compacta, whose dopamine neurons supply the striatum, and the pars reticulata, an output of the basal ganglia. It takes part in starting movement and in reward learning [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the striatum and the subthalamic nucleus; the pars compacta projects to the caudate and putamen, and the pars reticulata to the thalamus and superior colliculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Loss of its dopamine neurons causes Parkinson disease [purves2018].
