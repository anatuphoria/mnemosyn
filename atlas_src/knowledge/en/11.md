## Function
The two cerebral hemispheres: the cerebral cortex, the white matter beneath it and the basal nuclei deep inside. It is where most conscious perception, planning, language and memory take place [purves2018].

## Connections
The two hemispheres are joined by the corpus callosum and the anterior commissure; each communicates with the thalamus, basal nuclei, brainstem and spinal cord through the internal capsule [kandel2021].
