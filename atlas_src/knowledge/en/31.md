## Function
The upper part of the brainstem. It holds the nuclei of the oculomotor and trochlear nerves, the superior and inferior colliculi for visual and auditory reflexes, the substantia nigra and the ventral tegmental area [purves2018].

## Connections
The cerebral peduncles carry the corticospinal and corticopontine fibres on its front; the cerebral aqueduct runs through its back part [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage can cause a third nerve palsy with weakness of the opposite side (Weber syndrome), or problems of upward gaze when the dorsal midbrain is pressed [purves2018].
