## Function
The midbrain station of the auditory pathway, where almost all ascending sound information converges. It takes part in locating sounds and in startle and orienting reflexes [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the cochlear nuclei, the superior olive and the lateral lemniscus and projects to the medial geniculate nucleus [kandel2021].
