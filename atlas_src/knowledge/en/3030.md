## Function
The fibres from the occipital cortex to the pontine nuclei. They carry visual information to the cerebellum, used for example in tracking moving things with the eyes [kandel2021].

## Connections
They run through the back of the internal capsule and the lateral cerebral peduncle and end in the pontine nuclei [kandel2021].
