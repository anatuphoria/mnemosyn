## Function
A long bundle above the insula that joins the frontal lobe with the parietal lobe. It takes part in spatial attention, working memory and, on the left, language [kandel2021].

## Connections
It runs from the parietal cortex forward to the premotor and prefrontal cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage in the right hemisphere is linked with neglect of the left side of space [kandel2021].
