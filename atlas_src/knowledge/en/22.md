## Function
A small region below the thalamus that keeps the internal state of the body steady. It controls body temperature, thirst, hunger, sleep and waking, the stress response and reproduction, through the pituitary gland and the autonomic nervous system [purves2018].

## Connections
It controls the anterior pituitary through releasing hormones carried in the portal veins and sends axons into the posterior pituitary; it is linked with the limbic system and with brainstem and spinal autonomic centres [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Hypothalamic damage can disturb temperature, appetite, thirst, sleep and hormones, for example causing diabetes insipidus [christcrain2019].
