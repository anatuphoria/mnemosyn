## Function
A group of nuclei around the superior cerebellar peduncle in the pons. It relays taste, visceral and pain signals to the forebrain, takes part in breathing, and helps wake a person when something goes wrong, such as low oxygen [saper2005] [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the nucleus of the solitary tract and the dorsal horn and projects to the hypothalamus, amygdala and thalamus [kandel2021].
