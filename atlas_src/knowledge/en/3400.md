## Function
A tract in the Papez circuit that carries signals from the mammillary body to the anterior thalamus [papez1937].

## Connections
It runs from the mammillary body up through the hypothalamus to the anteroventral and other anterior nuclei of the thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to it, for example in thalamic stroke, can cause memory loss [kandel2021].
