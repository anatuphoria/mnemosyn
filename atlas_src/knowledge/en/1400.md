## Function
An almond shaped group of nuclei in the front of the medial temporal lobe. It detects threats and gives events their emotional weight, drives fear responses and helps store emotional memories [ledoux2000].

## Connections
It receives sensory input from the thalamus and cortex and projects to the hypothalamus and brainstem to trigger the body's fear responses, and to the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex [ledoux2000].

## Clinical
Damage on both sides reduces fear and the recognition of fear in faces; overactivity is linked with anxiety disorders [kandel2021].
