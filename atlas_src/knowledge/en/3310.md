## Function
The peduncle that joins the cerebellum with the medulla, carrying information about the body and balance [purves2018].

## Connections
It brings the dorsal spinocerebellar tract, climbing fibres from the inferior olive and vestibular fibres into the cerebellum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It is often damaged in lateral medullary stroke, which adds clumsiness of the limbs on the same side [purves2018].
