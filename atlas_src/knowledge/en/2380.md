## Function
Lobule X, the flocculus in the hemisphere. With the nodulus it forms the flocculonodular lobe, which keeps the eyes steady and maintains balance [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the vestibular nerve and vestibular nuclei and projects back to the vestibular nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes nystagmus and unsteady eye movements [kandel2021].
