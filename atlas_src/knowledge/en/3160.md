## Function
A bundle inside the cingulate and parahippocampal gyri. It joins the frontal, cingulate, parietal and medial temporal cortex and is part of the circuits for memory and emotion [papez1937] [kandel2021].

## Connections
It runs above the corpus callosum from the frontal lobe to the parahippocampal gyrus and entorhinal cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Changes in it are among the early white matter changes in Alzheimer disease [kandel2021].
