## Function
A nucleus in the preoptic area at the front of the hypothalamus. It takes part in sexual and parental behaviour and in body temperature, and differs in size between the sexes [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the amygdala, the bed nucleus of the stria terminalis and other hypothalamic nuclei [kandel2021].
