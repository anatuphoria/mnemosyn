## Function
The butterfly shaped core of the spinal cord, made of nerve cell bodies. It holds the dorsal horn for sensation, the intermediate zone for autonomic and interneurons, and the ventral horn for motor neurons [purves2018].

## Connections
Dorsal roots bring sensory fibres into its dorsal horn; ventral roots carry motor axons out of its ventral horn [kandel2021].
