## Function
The nerve that moves the eye upward, downward and inward, raises the eyelid, constricts the pupil and focuses the lens [purves2018].

## Connections
It leaves the front of the midbrain between the cerebral peduncles and passes through the cavernous sinus to the orbit [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A palsy causes a drooping eyelid and an eye turned down and out; a dilated pupil can be the first sign of an aneurysm or of brain swelling pressing on the nerve [purves2018].
