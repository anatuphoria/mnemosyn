## Function
Required for forming new declarative memories of facts and events, and important for spatial memory [kandel2021].

## Connections
Its main cortical input comes from the entorhinal cortex through the perforant path; its output leaves mainly through the fornix and back to the entorhinal cortex [purves2018].

## Clinical
Bilateral removal of the medial temporal lobes, including the hippocampi, caused a severe and lasting inability to form new memories in the patient H.M. [scoville1957]. The neurofibrillary changes of Alzheimer disease appear early in the entorhinal region and hippocampus [braak1991].
