## Function
The lowest part of the brainstem, continuous with the spinal cord. It holds centres for breathing, heart rate and blood pressure, and the nuclei for swallowing, the voice and the tongue [purves2018].

## Connections
Its pyramids carry the corticospinal tract, which crosses at the pyramidal decussation; the medial lemniscus begins here after the dorsal column nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Lateral medullary stroke (Wallenberg syndrome) causes dizziness, trouble swallowing, loss of pain sensation on the face of one side and the body of the other side, and clumsiness [purves2018].
