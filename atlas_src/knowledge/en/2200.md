## Function
The largest serotonin nucleus, in the midbrain below the aqueduct. It supplies serotonin to much of the forebrain and takes part in mood, sleep and the response to stress [berger2009].

## Connections
It projects to the cortex, striatum, amygdala and thalamus, and receives from the prefrontal cortex, habenula and hypothalamus [kandel2021].
