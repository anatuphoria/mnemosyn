## Function
The front part of the orbital surface of the frontal lobe. As part of the orbitofrontal cortex it takes part in evaluating outcomes and guiding choices [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the other orbitofrontal gyri, the amygdala and the ventral striatum, and reaches the temporal pole through the uncinate fasciculus [kandel2021].
