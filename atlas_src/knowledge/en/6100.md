## Function
The deep region supplied by the posterior choroidal and thalamoperforating arteries: much of the thalamus, including its medial and posterior parts, and the choroid plexus [kandel2021].

## Connections
These small arteries arise from the first parts of the posterior cerebral artery and enter the thalamus from below and behind [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Infarcts here can cause sensory loss of the opposite side, memory problems or drowsiness, depending on which thalamic nuclei are hit [purves2018].
