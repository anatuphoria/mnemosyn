## Function
The motor area on the medial surface in front of the leg region of the primary motor cortex. It takes part in planning and starting movements generated from within rather than by an outside cue, and in sequences of movements [kandel2021].

## Connections
It sends fibres to the primary motor cortex and into the corticospinal tract, and receives input from the basal nuclei through the ventral anterior and ventral lateral thalamus [purves2018].

## Clinical
Damage can cause a lack of spontaneous movement and speech on the opposite side, which usually improves over weeks [kandel2021].
