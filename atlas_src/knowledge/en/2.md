## Function
The part of the central nervous system inside the skull: the forebrain, the brainstem and the cerebellum. It holds the circuits for perception, movement, memory, emotion and the control of the internal organs [purves2018].

## Connections
It continues into the spinal cord at the foramen magnum and is linked to the head and neck by twelve pairs of cranial nerves [kandel2021].
