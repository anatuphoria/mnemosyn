## Function
The part of the medial superior frontal gyrus that turns onto the orbital surface. It belongs to the ventromedial prefrontal cortex, which takes part in judging value and guiding decisions [damasio1994].

## Connections
It is linked with the amygdala, hypothalamus and cingulate cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the ventromedial prefrontal cortex leaves intelligence largely intact but impairs social behaviour and decision making, as in Phineas Gage [damasio1994].
