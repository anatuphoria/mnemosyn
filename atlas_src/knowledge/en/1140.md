## Function
The back part of the orbital surface of the frontal lobe, close to the olfactory cortex. It takes part in the orbitofrontal processing of smell and taste and their reward value [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the olfactory and taste cortex, the amygdala and the hypothalamus [kandel2021].
