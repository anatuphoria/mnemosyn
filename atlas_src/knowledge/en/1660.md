## Function
The lower gyrus of the lateral temporal lobe. It is a late stage of the ventral visual stream, where objects are recognised regardless of size or position [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the occipital visual areas through the inferior longitudinal fasciculus and projects to the medial temporal lobe and prefrontal cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage can impair the recognition of objects (visual agnosia) [kandel2021].
