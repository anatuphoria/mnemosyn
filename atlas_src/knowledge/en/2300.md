## Function
The first part of the large ansiform lobule in the cerebellar hemisphere. Together with crus II it is linked with the prefrontal and parietal cortex and takes part in cognition and language [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the prefrontal cortex through the pontine nuclei and projects through the dentate nucleus [kandel2021].
