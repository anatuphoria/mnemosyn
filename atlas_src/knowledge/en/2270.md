## Function
The overall outline of the medulla, shown as a surface in which its nuclei and tracts sit. It is the lowest part of the brainstem, between the pons and the spinal cord [purves2018].

## Connections
It carries the pyramids and olives in front and the gracile and cuneate tubercles behind [kandel2021].
