## Function
Lobule IX of the cerebellar hemisphere, next to the flocculonodular lobe. It forms the cerebellar tonsil on the underside of the cerebellum [kandel2021].

## Connections
Like the rest of the posterior lobe it projects through the deep cerebellar nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The cerebellar tonsils can be pushed down through the foramen magnum when pressure in the skull rises, which can stop breathing [purves2018].
