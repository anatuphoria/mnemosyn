## Function
The spinal cord divided by the spinal nerves that leave it: 8 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar and 5 sacral segments, plus a small coccygeal segment. Each segment serves a band of skin (dermatome) and a set of muscles (myotome) [purves2018].

## Connections
Because the cord is shorter than the vertebral column, the lower segments lie higher than their vertebrae, and their roots run down as the cauda equina [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The level of a spinal injury is named by the lowest segment that still works normally [purves2018].
