## Function
The overall outline of the pons, shown as a surface in which its nuclei and tracts sit. It is the middle part of the brainstem, between the midbrain and the medulla [purves2018].

## Connections
It carries the pontine nuclei in front and the tegmentum behind, under the fourth ventricle [kandel2021].
