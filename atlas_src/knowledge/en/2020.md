## Function
The pars reticulata of the substantia nigra, an output nucleus of the basal ganglia like the internal globus pallidus. Its inhibitory neurons control eye movements and movements of the head and body [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the striatum and subthalamic nucleus and projects to the ventral anterior and ventral lateral thalamus and to the superior colliculus [kandel2021].
