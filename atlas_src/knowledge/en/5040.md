## Function
The fourth cervical segment. It carries sensation from the top of the shoulders and is the main source of the phrenic nerve to the diaphragm [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the cervical plexus and the phrenic nerve [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Injury at or above this segment can paralyse the diaphragm, so that a person needs help to breathe [purves2018].
