## Function
A thalamic nucleus that relays output of the cerebellum, and some of the basal ganglia, to the motor cortex [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the dentate and interposed nuclei through the superior cerebellar peduncle and projects to the primary motor and premotor cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
It is the target for deep brain stimulation or lesions to treat essential tremor [kandel2021].
