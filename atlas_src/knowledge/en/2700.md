## Function
The master gland of the endocrine system. Its anterior lobe releases growth hormone, prolactin, thyroid-stimulating hormone, adrenocorticotropic hormone and the gonadotropins under the control of hypothalamic releasing and inhibiting hormones; its posterior lobe releases oxytocin and vasopressin made in the hypothalamus [purves2018].

## Connections
Sits in the sella turcica below the hypothalamus, joined to it by the pituitary stalk. Hypothalamic hormones reach the anterior lobe through the hypophyseal portal veins; axons from the supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei run down the stalk and end in the posterior lobe [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A pituitary adenoma can press upward on the optic chiasm and take both temporal visual fields (bitemporal hemianopia), and can cause too much or too little of its hormones; loss of vasopressin release causes diabetes insipidus [kandel2021].
