## Function
A group of dopamine neurons in the midbrain next to the substantia nigra. It signals rewards that are better or worse than expected, which drives learning and motivation [schultz1997].

## Connections
It projects to the nucleus accumbens, the prefrontal cortex and the limbic system (the mesolimbic and mesocortical pathways) [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Addictive drugs act on its pathway to the nucleus accumbens; its projections are involved in schizophrenia and are the target of antipsychotic drugs [kandel2021].
