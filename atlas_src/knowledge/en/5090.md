## Function
The first thoracic segment. It carries sensation from the inner upper arm and supplies the small muscles of the hand; it also holds sympathetic neurons for the eye and face [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the lower trunk of the brachial plexus, and its sympathetic fibres rise through the sympathetic chain to the head [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to its sympathetic outflow causes Horner syndrome: a small pupil and drooping eyelid on the same side [purves2018].
