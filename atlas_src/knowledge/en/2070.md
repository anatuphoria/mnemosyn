## Function
A group of mainly cholinergic neurons at the junction of the midbrain and pons. It takes part in arousal, rapid eye movement sleep and the control of walking and posture [saper2005] [kandel2021].

## Connections
It projects to the thalamus, the basal ganglia and the brainstem and spinal motor networks, and receives from the globus pallidus and substantia nigra [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Its neurons are lost in Parkinson disease, and it has been explored as a stimulation target for freezing of gait [kandel2021].
