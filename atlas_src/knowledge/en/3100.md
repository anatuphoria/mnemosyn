## Function
A curved bundle that joins the language areas of the temporal and parietal lobes with Broca's area in the frontal lobe [purves2018].

## Connections
It arches around the end of the lateral sulcus from the superior and middle temporal gyri to the inferior frontal gyrus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage in the dominant hemisphere is a classic cause of conduction aphasia, with poor repetition but fair understanding [purves2018].
