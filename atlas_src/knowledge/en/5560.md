## Function
The spinal part of the pathway for pain, temperature and crude touch from the opposite side of the body [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres start in the dorsal horn, cross in the anterior white commissure and ascend in the lateral column toward the thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to one side of the cord causes loss of pain and temperature on the opposite side below the lesion (the Brown-Séquard pattern) [purves2018].
