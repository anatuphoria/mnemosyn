## Function
The lobule on the medial surface where the precentral and postcentral gyri meet. It holds the motor and sensory representation of the leg and foot and contributes to bladder control [penfield1937] [purves2018].

## Connections
It sends fibres into the corticospinal tract and receives from the ventral posterolateral nucleus of the thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage, as in a stroke of the anterior cerebral artery, causes weakness and sensory loss mainly in the opposite leg [purves2018].
