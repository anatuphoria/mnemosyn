## Function
The insular cortex, supplied by branches of the middle cerebral artery that run over it in the lateral sulcus [kandel2021].

## Connections
The middle cerebral artery divides over the insula, and short branches enter the insular cortex [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The insula is involved in most large middle cerebral artery strokes [purves2018].
