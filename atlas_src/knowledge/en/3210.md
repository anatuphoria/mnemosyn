## Function
The largest commissure, with hundreds of millions of fibres joining corresponding areas of the two hemispheres [purves2018].

## Connections
Its genu joins the frontal lobes, its body the frontal and parietal lobes, and its splenium the temporal and occipital lobes [kandel2021].

## Clinical
After it is cut to treat epilepsy, information seen or felt by one hemisphere is not available to the other, which shows up in careful testing [purves2018].
