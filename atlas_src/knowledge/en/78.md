## Function
The nerves that leave the brain and brainstem directly. They carry smell, vision, hearing, balance and taste, sensation of the face, and commands to the eye, face, jaw, throat and tongue muscles and to the internal organs [purves2018].

## Connections
All but the olfactory and optic nerves attach to the brainstem; their nuclei lie in the midbrain, pons and medulla [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The pattern of cranial nerve signs helps locate a lesion in the brainstem [purves2018].
