## Function
The grey matter around the cerebral aqueduct. It organises defensive responses and controls pain by sending signals that dampen pain transmission in the spinal cord [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the prefrontal cortex, amygdala and hypothalamus and projects to the rostral ventromedial medulla and raphe nuclei, which reach the dorsal horn [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Opioid drugs relieve pain partly by acting in this region [purves2018].
