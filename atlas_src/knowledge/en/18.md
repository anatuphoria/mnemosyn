## Function
The grey matter nuclei deep in the hemispheres: the caudate nucleus, putamen, globus pallidus and nucleus accumbens, here grouped with the amygdala. With the subthalamic nucleus and substantia nigra, the basal ganglia select and start wanted movements and suppress unwanted ones [purves2018].

## Connections
They receive from almost all of the cortex and send their output through the thalamus back to the frontal lobe, forming loops for movement, eye movements, cognition and motivation [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Disorders of the basal ganglia cause too little movement, as in Parkinson disease, or too much, as in Huntington disease [purves2018].
