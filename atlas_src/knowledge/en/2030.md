## Function
A round nucleus in the midbrain, reddish in fresh tissue from its iron content. It takes part in the control of limb movement and in the circuit between the cerebellum and inferior olive [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the cerebellum through the superior cerebellar peduncle and from the motor cortex, and projects to the spinal cord through the rubrospinal tract and to the inferior olive through the central tegmental tract [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Lesions in its region can cause a coarse tremor and a third nerve palsy (Benedikt syndrome) [purves2018].
