## Overview
Noradrenaline comes mainly from the locus coeruleus, a small nucleus in the pons whose branching axons reach nearly the whole brain. It sets the level of arousal and shifts attention between focused and broad modes [astonjones2005].

## Step 1
Role: The locus coeruleus fires slowly in sleep, steadily when awake and in bursts when something important happens, adjusting how strongly the brain responds [astonjones2005].
Disorders: Overactivity is linked to anxiety, panic and the hyperarousal of PTSD; the nucleus is among the first sites damaged in Alzheimer's disease [kandel2021].
Drugs: Clonidine reduces its firing and is used for withdrawal symptoms and ADHD [kandel2021].

## Step 2
Role: Its axons run forward in the dorsal noradrenergic bundle to the thalamus and cortex [purves2018].
Disorders: Damage to ascending arousal fibres can lower alertness [kandel2021].

## Step 3
Role: Noradrenaline in the thalamus helps switch it from sleep rhythms to waking relay [saper2005].
Disorders: Weak arousal input to the thalamus contributes to drowsiness and reduced consciousness [saper2005].

## Step 4
Role: In frontal and parietal cortex it sharpens attention and working memory [astonjones2005].
Disorders: ADHD is linked to weak prefrontal noradrenaline and dopamine signalling [kandel2021].
Drugs: Atomoxetine and methylphenidate raise noradrenaline here and improve attention [kandel2021].

## Step 5
Role: In the hippocampus and amygdala it strengthens memories of emotional and stressful events [kandel2021].
Disorders: Overly strong fear memories in PTSD are linked to this effect [kandel2021].
Drugs: Propranolol blocks beta receptors and is being studied as a way to weaken traumatic memories [kandel2021].

## Step 6
Role: It drives the paraventricular nucleus to start the hormonal stress response [ulrichlai2009].
Disorders: Chronic stress keeps this drive high [ulrichlai2009].

## Step 7
Role: Projections to the cerebellum and spinal cord help adjust movement to the level of arousal [purves2018].
Disorders: Stress and anxiety increase physiological tremor through noradrenaline [kandel2021].
Drugs: Propranolol reduces stress-related tremor and the bodily symptoms of performance anxiety [kandel2021].
