## Function
The part of the superior frontal gyrus on the medial surface of the hemisphere. It belongs to the medial prefrontal cortex and is engaged in self referential thought and the control of behaviour [miller2001].

## Connections
It is linked with the cingulate cortex through the cingulum and with the mediodorsal nucleus of the thalamus [kandel2021].
