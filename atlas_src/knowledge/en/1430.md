## Function
The inner part of the lentiform nucleus, made of an external and an internal segment. The internal segment is the main output of the basal ganglia and holds the thalamus in check with inhibitory neurons [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the striatum and the subthalamic nucleus and projects to the ventral anterior and ventral lateral nuclei of the thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Its internal segment is a target for deep brain stimulation or surgery in Parkinson disease and dystonia [kandel2021].
