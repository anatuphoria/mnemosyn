## Function
The nucleus of the solitary tract and the dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus. The first receives taste and signals from the internal organs; the second sends parasympathetic commands to the heart, lungs and gut [purves2018].

## Connections
The solitary nucleus receives from the facial, glossopharyngeal and vagus nerves and projects to the parabrachial nucleus, hypothalamus and brainstem reflex centres; the dorsal motor nucleus sends its axons in the vagus nerve [kandel2021].
