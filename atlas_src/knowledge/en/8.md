## Function
The five sacral segments at the lowest end of the spinal cord, in the conus medullaris. They serve the back of the legs, the sole of the foot and the skin around the anus and genitals, and hold the parasympathetic neurons for the bladder, bowel and sexual organs [purves2018].

## Connections
They lie behind the first lumbar vertebra; their roots run down in the cauda equina and form the sacral plexus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to these segments or their roots causes loss of bladder and bowel control and numbness of the saddle area [purves2018].
