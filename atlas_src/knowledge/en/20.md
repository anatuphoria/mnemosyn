## Function
The part of the forebrain between the hemispheres and the midbrain, around the third ventricle: the thalamus, hypothalamus, subthalamus and epithalamus. It relays information to the cortex and controls the endocrine and autonomic systems [purves2018].

## Connections
Its thalamus is linked with the whole cortex, and its hypothalamus controls the pituitary gland through the pituitary stalk [kandel2021].
