## Function
The parietal cortex supplied by the middle cerebral artery: the somatosensory cortex for the face and arm and the inferior parietal lobule [purves2018].

## Connections
Its branches come from the middle cerebral artery as it leaves the lateral sulcus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct here causes sensory loss of the opposite face and arm and, in the right hemisphere, neglect of the left side of space [purves2018].
