## Function
A thalamic nucleus that relays output of the basal ganglia to the frontal motor areas, taking part in planning and starting movement [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the internal globus pallidus and the substantia nigra pars reticulata and projects to the premotor cortex, supplementary motor area and prefrontal cortex [kandel2021].
