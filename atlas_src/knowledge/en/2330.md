## Function
Lobules IV and V of the anterior lobe. They hold the main cerebellar map of the body and coordinate limb movement [purves2018].

## Connections
They receive from the spinal cord and the motor cortex through the pontine nuclei and project through the interposed and dentate nuclei [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the anterior lobe, as in chronic alcohol use, causes an unsteady broad based gait [purves2018].
