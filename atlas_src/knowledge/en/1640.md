## Function
The middle gyrus of the lateral temporal lobe. It takes part in word meaning and, near its back end with the occipital lobe, in seeing motion [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the frontal lobe through the arcuate fasciculus and the inferior fronto-occipital fasciculus, and with the occipital lobe [kandel2021].
