## Function
The main sensory nerve of the face, mouth and front of the head, and the motor nerve of the chewing muscles [purves2018].

## Connections
It attaches to the side of the pons; its sensory fibres end in the trigeminal nuclei, which run from the midbrain into the upper cervical cord [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Trigeminal neuralgia causes brief, severe stabbing pain in part of the face [purves2018].
