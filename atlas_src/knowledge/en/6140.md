## Function
The lower cerebellum and the side of the lower pons and medulla, supplied by the anterior inferior and posterior inferior cerebellar arteries [purves2018].

## Connections
The posterior inferior cerebellar artery arises from the vertebral artery and the anterior inferior cerebellar artery from the lower basilar artery [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A blockage of the posterior inferior cerebellar artery causes lateral medullary (Wallenberg) syndrome; a blockage of the anterior inferior cerebellar artery can add deafness on the same side [purves2018].
