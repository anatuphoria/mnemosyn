## Function
A tract near the midline of the brainstem that joins the nuclei for eye movement with the vestibular nuclei, so that both eyes move together [purves2018].

## Connections
It joins the abducens nucleus of one side with the oculomotor nucleus of the other and carries vestibular signals to the eye muscle nuclei and the neck [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes internuclear ophthalmoplegia: on looking to the side, the eye moving inward lags behind; it is a common sign of multiple sclerosis [purves2018].
