## Function
The medial part of the dorsal column. It carries fine touch, vibration and position sense from the legs and lower body [purves2018].

## Connections
It is made of dorsal root fibres from the lower thoracic, lumbar and sacral segments that ascend on the same side to the gracile nucleus of the medulla [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of position sense in the legs, so that a person sways when standing with the eyes closed [purves2018].
