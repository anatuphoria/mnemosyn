## Function
The front part of each hemisphere, in front of the central sulcus. It holds the primary motor cortex and premotor areas, Broca's area for speech production in the dominant hemisphere, and prefrontal cortex for planning, working memory and control of behaviour [purves2018] [miller2001].

## Connections
Its prefrontal areas are linked with the mediodorsal nucleus of the thalamus, the basal nuclei and the association cortex of the other lobes; its motor areas send fibres into the corticospinal tract [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Frontal damage can cause weakness of the opposite side, loss of fluent speech, and changes in judgment, planning and personality, as in the case of Phineas Gage [damasio1994].
