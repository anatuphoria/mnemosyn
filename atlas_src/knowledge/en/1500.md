## Function
The cortex along the calcarine sulcus: the primary visual cortex (V1). It maps the opposite half of the visual field, with the centre of gaze at the back and a large share of the area [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives the optic radiation from the lateral geniculate nucleus and projects to the surrounding visual association areas [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes a loss of the opposite visual field in both eyes; when the posterior cerebral artery is blocked, central vision is often spared [purves2018].
