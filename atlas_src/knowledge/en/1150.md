## Function
The lateral part of the orbital surface of the frontal lobe. As part of the orbitofrontal cortex it helps to stop responses that are no longer rewarded [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the inferior frontal gyrus, the amygdala and the ventral striatum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Orbitofrontal damage can cause impulsive and socially inappropriate behaviour [damasio1994].
