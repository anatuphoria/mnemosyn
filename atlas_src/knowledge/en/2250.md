## Function
The overall outline of the midbrain, shown as a surface in which its nuclei and tracts sit. It is the upper part of the brainstem, between the diencephalon and the pons [purves2018].

## Connections
It carries the cerebral peduncles in front and the colliculi behind, with the cerebral aqueduct between them [kandel2021].
