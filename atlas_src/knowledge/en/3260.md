## Function
The pathway for pain, temperature and crude touch from the opposite side of the body [purves2018].

## Connections
It starts in the dorsal horn, crosses in the spinal cord within a segment or two, ascends in the lateral part of the cord and brainstem and ends in the thalamus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes loss of pain and temperature on the opposite side below the lesion; cutting it was once used to relieve severe pain [purves2018].
