## Function
The main output tract of the hippocampus, a link in the circuit for memory [papez1937] [squire2004].

## Connections
It arches from the hippocampus under the corpus callosum to the mammillary bodies and septal nuclei, and carries cholinergic fibres from the septum back to the hippocampus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to both fornices, for example during surgery near the third ventricle, can cause severe loss of memory for new events [kandel2021].
