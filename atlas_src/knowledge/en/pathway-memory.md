## Overview
The brain keeps several kinds of memory. Facts and events depend on the hippocampus and nearby cortex while they are being stored and are later held in the cortex; habits and skills depend on the striatum and cerebellum [squire2004].

## Step 1
Role: Temporal association cortex processes what we perceive and later holds the long-term traces of facts and events [squire2004].
Disorders: Damage to the lateral temporal cortex causes loss of knowledge about words and objects (semantic dementia) [kandel2021].

## Step 2
Role: The parahippocampal gyrus and entorhinal cortex gather information from the cortex and pass it to the hippocampus [squire2004].
Disorders: The entorhinal cortex is among the first areas damaged in Alzheimer's disease [braak1991].

## Step 3
Role: The hippocampus binds the parts of an experience into a new memory [squire2004].
Disorders: Patient HM could no longer form new memories after both hippocampi were removed, while old memories and skills remained [scoville1957].
Drugs: Benzodiazepines and alcohol in high doses block the storage of new memories (blackouts) [kandel2021].

## Step 4
Role: The fornix carries hippocampal output to the mammillary bodies, part of the circuit needed for recall [squire2004].
Disorders: Thiamine deficiency damages the mammillary bodies and causes Korsakoff amnesia [kandel2021].
Drugs: Thiamine given early prevents and treats Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome [kandel2021].

## Step 5
Role: Over time memories are reorganised so that the medial prefrontal cortex and other cortex can retrieve them with less help from the hippocampus [squire2004].
Disorders: Frontal damage causes problems with recalling the source and order of events [kandel2021].

## Step 6
Role: The amygdala strengthens memories of emotional events, which is why they are vivid [kandel2021].
Disorders: Overly strong emotional memories contribute to PTSD [kandel2021].

## Step 7
Role: Acetylcholine from the nucleus basalis supports attention and the storage of new memories [hasselmo2006].
Disorders: Its loss contributes to the memory problems of Alzheimer's disease [kandel2021].
Drugs: Cholinesterase inhibitors such as donepezil are used to support memory in Alzheimer's disease [kandel2021].

## Step 8
Role: The striatum and cerebellum store habits and motor skills, which do not need the hippocampus [squire2004].
Disorders: Parkinson's disease impairs habit learning, while amnesic patients like HM can still learn new skills [squire2004].
