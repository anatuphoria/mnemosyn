## Function
A hook shaped bundle that joins the front of the temporal lobe with the orbitofrontal cortex. It takes part in emotion, memory for names and social behaviour [kandel2021].

## Connections
It curves around the lateral sulcus from the temporal pole and amygdala region to the orbital and inferior frontal gyri [kandel2021].
