## Function
The tenth thoracic segment. It carries sensation from the band of skin at the level of the navel [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres form the tenth intercostal nerve; its lateral horn holds sympathetic neurons [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The navel is a useful landmark for the T10 sensory level [purves2018].
