## Function
A large paired mass of nuclei above the hypothalamus. Almost all information on its way to the cortex passes through a thalamic nucleus, including every sense except smell, and output of the cerebellum and basal ganglia [purves2018].

## Connections
Each thalamic nucleus has its own cortical target, and the cortex sends fibres back to the same nucleus; the thalamic reticular nucleus regulates the traffic [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Thalamic strokes can cause sensory loss of the opposite side and, sometimes, severe burning pain later (central post stroke pain) [purves2018].
