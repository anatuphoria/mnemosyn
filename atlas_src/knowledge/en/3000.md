## Function
The main pathway for voluntary movement, especially skilled movement of the hands and fingers. It carries commands from the motor cortex to the spinal cord [purves2018].

## Connections
It starts in the primary motor cortex and nearby areas, runs through the internal capsule, cerebral peduncle, pons and medullary pyramid, and most of its fibres cross at the pyramidal decussation to form the lateral corticospinal tract [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage above the decussation causes weakness of the opposite side with upper motor neuron signs; damage in the spinal cord causes weakness on the same side below the lesion [purves2018].
