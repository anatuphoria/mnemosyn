## Function
One of the orbital gyri on the underside of the frontal lobe. The orbitofrontal cortex judges the reward value of smells, tastes and other outcomes and updates behaviour when that value changes [kandel2021].

## Connections
It is linked with the amygdala, the ventral striatum, the hypothalamus and the mediodorsal nucleus of the thalamus [kandel2021].
