## Function
The folded sheet of grey matter on the surface of the hemispheres, a few millimetres thick. Most of it is neocortex with six layers, organised into sensory, motor and association areas [purves2018].

## Connections
Each area exchanges fibres with the thalamus, sends output to the basal nuclei, brainstem and spinal cord, and is joined to other areas by association fibres in the same hemisphere and commissural fibres to the other [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the cortex causes deficits that depend on where it lies, such as weakness, sensory loss, loss of language (aphasia) or neglect of one side of space [purves2018].
