## Function
The oral part of the pontine reticular nucleus, in the upper pontine tegmentum. It takes part in arousal and in generating rapid eye movement sleep [saper2005] [kandel2021].

## Connections
It projects to the spinal cord through the reticulospinal tract and to other brainstem reticular nuclei [kandel2021].
