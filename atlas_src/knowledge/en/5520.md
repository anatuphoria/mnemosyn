## Function
The main descending tract for voluntary movement in the spinal cord, made of the corticospinal fibres that crossed in the medulla [purves2018].

## Connections
It runs in the lateral column and ends on motor neurons and interneurons along the cord, most heavily in the segments for the arms and legs [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage causes weakness and upper motor neuron signs on the same side below the lesion [purves2018].
