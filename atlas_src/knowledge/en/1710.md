## Function
The superior parietal lobule, behind the postcentral gyrus. It combines touch, position sense and vision to build a map of the body and guide reaching and grasping [kandel2021].

## Connections
It receives from the somatosensory cortex and the visual dorsal stream and is linked with the premotor and prefrontal cortex through the superior longitudinal fasciculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage can cause clumsy, misdirected reaching to visual targets (optic ataxia) [kandel2021].
