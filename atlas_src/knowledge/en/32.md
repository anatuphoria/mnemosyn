## Function
The middle part of the brainstem, with a large bulge in front made of the pontine nuclei. It relays signals from the cortex to the cerebellum and holds the nuclei of the trigeminal, abducens, facial and vestibulocochlear nerves, and centres for breathing and sleep [purves2018].

## Connections
The middle cerebellar peduncle carries the pontocerebellar fibres to the opposite cerebellum; the corticospinal tract runs through its basal part [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A large pontine stroke can leave a person conscious but unable to move anything but the eyes (locked in syndrome) [purves2018].
