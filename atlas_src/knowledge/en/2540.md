## Function
A large area in the side of the hypothalamus. Its neurons promote waking, feeding and seeking rewards; some make orexin (hypocretin), which keeps a person awake [thannickal2000] [morton2006].

## Connections
It projects widely to the cortex and to brainstem arousal centres, and receives from the arcuate nucleus and limbic system [saper2005].

## Clinical
Loss of its orexin neurons causes narcolepsy [thannickal2000].
