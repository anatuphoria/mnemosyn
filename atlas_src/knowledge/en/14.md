## Function
The upper back part of each hemisphere, behind the central sulcus. It holds the primary somatosensory cortex and association areas that combine touch, vision and body position to guide attention and movement in space [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the ventral posterior and lateral posterior thalamus and the pulvinar, and is linked with the frontal lobe through the superior longitudinal fasciculus [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the right parietal lobe often causes neglect of the left side of space; damage to the left can disturb language, calculation and skilled movement [purves2018].
