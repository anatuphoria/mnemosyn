## Function
A large folded nucleus in the medulla that sends climbing fibres to the cerebellum. These fibres signal errors that help the cerebellum learn movements [purves2018].

## Connections
It receives from the red nucleus through the central tegmental tract and from the spinal cord, and projects to the opposite cerebellum through the inferior cerebellar peduncle [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Damage to the circuit between the dentate nucleus, red nucleus and inferior olive can cause palatal tremor [kandel2021].
