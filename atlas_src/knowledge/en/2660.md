## Function
A group of large cholinergic neurons in the basal forebrain. It is the main source of acetylcholine for the cerebral cortex and supports attention and learning [hasselmo2006].

## Connections
It projects to the whole cortex and to the amygdala, and receives from the limbic system and brainstem [kandel2021].

## Clinical
Its neurons are lost early in Alzheimer disease, the basis for treating the disease with drugs that slow the breakdown of acetylcholine [hasselmo2006].
