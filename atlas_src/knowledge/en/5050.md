## Function
The fifth cervical segment. It carries sensation from the outer upper arm and supplies the deltoid and the biceps, which lift and bend the arm [purves2018].

## Connections
Its fibres join the upper trunk of the brachial plexus and the phrenic nerve [kandel2021].

## Clinical
The biceps reflex tests segments C5 and C6 [purves2018].
