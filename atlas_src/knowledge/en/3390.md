## Function
The nerve to the superior oblique muscle, which turns the eye down and in [purves2018].

## Connections
It is the only cranial nerve to leave the back of the brainstem; it crosses before leaving the midbrain and runs around it to the cavernous sinus and orbit [kandel2021].

## Clinical
A palsy causes double vision on looking down, as when reading or walking downstairs, and a head tilt away from the affected side [purves2018].
