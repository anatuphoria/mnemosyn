## Function
The upper surface of the cerebellum, the dentate nucleus and part of the upper pons and midbrain, supplied by the superior cerebellar artery [purves2018].

## Connections
The artery arises near the top of the basilar artery and winds around the brainstem to the top of the cerebellum [kandel2021].

## Clinical
An infarct causes clumsiness of the limbs on the same side and an unsteady gait [purves2018].
