# Experiments

Each experiment is a question you can answer in a few minutes with Mnemosyn. Read the question,
make a prediction, open the experiment, and only then unfold the answer. Every result stated here
is checked against the model or the atlas by a test in the project's test suite, so a change that
would make a page wrong fails the tests.
{ .lede }

## In the Lab

1. [Central or nephrogenic?](central-or-nephrogenic.md) Two people make dilute urine. Does
   desmopressin help both?
2. [A night without water](a-night-without-water.md) What does the urine do after eight hours
   without water, in health and in central diabetes insipidus?
3. [Too much water](too-much-water.md) SIADH and primary polydipsia both mean too much water. Why
   does only one lower sodium?
4. [The dexamethasone tests](dexamethasone-tests.md) How do two doses of one drug tell three causes
   of high cortisol apart?
5. [Addison's disease](addisons-disease.md) When the adrenal cortex fails, what happens to ACTH?
6. [Stopping a steroid course](stopping-a-steroid-course.md) Why must weeks of hydrocortisone be
   stopped slowly?

## In Lesion and Pathways

1. [One artery, many structures](one-artery-many-structures.md) What does one blocked branch of the
   middle cerebral artery take with it?
2. [Which side?](which-side.md) A stroke in the left hemisphere weakens which side of the body, and
   why?

## Opening a Lab experiment

Each Lab experiment comes as a small `.mnemolab` file. Download it, then in Mnemosyn switch to the
**Lab** and choose **File, Open** (`Ctrl+O`). The experiment opens in the system it was made for.
