# Settings

A few choices about how Mnemosyn looks and starts, kept on your computer between sessions.
{ .lede }

Choose **File, Settings**, or press `Ctrl+,` (on a Mac it is under the **Mnemosyn** menu). Every choice is saved as you make it, so there is nothing to confirm; **Close** just closes
the window.

## Appearance

**Theme** is **Dark** or **Light**. Dark is the theme Mnemosyn was drawn for. Light uses the same
layout on white, for a bright room or a projector. The theme changes the next time Mnemosyn
starts. MRI slices stay on black in both themes, the way scans are read.

## Lab timeline

Four parts of the Lab timeline can be switched off when you want a plainer picture, for example
for a screenshot in your notes:

1. the normal line behind each value,
2. the guide lines where a change starts and ends,
3. the values shown under the pointer,
4. the whole run bar above the ruler.

The first two are also in the timeline's right click menu. All four are remembered.

## When Mnemosyn starts

You can bring back the notice about medical use if you ticked "do not show again", and you can ask
for the welcome tour at the next start. The tour is also under **Help,
Welcome tour** at any time.

## Where it is kept

Settings live with the rest of your local data and never leave your computer. The Privacy section
of the Download page says what Mnemosyn stores.
