# Mnemosyn { .visually-hidden }

A free 3D atlas of the human brain and spinal cord, with a lab for the body's control systems.
Turn it, slice it, follow a pathway through it, test yourself on it, and change a body system to
see what follows. Made for students; every shape and sentence cites its source.

[Download](download.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary } [What is inside](atlas/structures.md){ .md-button }

</div>

## A closer look { .section-title }

<div class="closer" markdown>

<div markdown>
![The Slices workspace: an axial slice with coloured labels over an MRI template](assets/screenshot-slices.png){ .shot }

**Slices.** Labels drawn sharp over a smooth MRI, in neurological convention. Hover to name, click
to select, and centre the slice on any structure with one key.
</div>

<div markdown>
![The stress axis route with its schematic hormone timeline](assets/screenshot-activity.png){ .shot }

**Pathways.** Each station lights in 3D and in the slice. The stress axis, the body clock and
hunger also play a schematic hormone timeline.
</div>

<div markdown>
![The Lab after cutting the pituitary stalk, with the Why chain and the timeline](assets/screenshot-lab.png){ .shot }

**The Lab.** The brain colours each part by how far it is from normal, and Why explains the
difference as a numbered chain of causes, each with its sources. [How it works](guide.md#the-lab)
</div>
